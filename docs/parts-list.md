# Parts list

Everything here is plug-and-play. No soldering.

| # | Item | Qty | Approx. price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **M5Stack M5PaperS3** | 1 | ~$59 (M5Stack list price) | The reader itself: screen, battery, case. Make sure it's the **PaperS3** (ESP32-S3), not the original "M5Paper". |
| 2 | microSD card, 8–32 GB | 1 | ~$8 | Any reputable brand. Format as **FAT32**. Books live here. |
| 3 | USB-C cable (data, not charge-only) | 1 | ~$5 | Needed for flashing. You may already have one. |
| 4 | Plain 5 V USB charger | 1 | ~$8 | ⚠️ **Avoid Quick Charge (QC 2.0/3.0) chargers.** Retailers warn they can push a higher voltage and damage the PaperS3. A basic 5 V / 1 A or 2 A brick is ideal. |
| 5 | *(optional)* Protective sleeve / pouch | 1 | ~$10 | Something ~5" sized so it lives in a bag or pocket safely. |
| 6 | *(optional)* Spare microSD for backup | 1 | ~$8 | Keep a copy of her library. |

**Core cost: ~$70–80** (items 1–4).
